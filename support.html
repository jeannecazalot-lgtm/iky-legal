The quiz for two: who knows the other best?

How to play

One of you creates a game and gets a game code. The other joins with that code. Answer the same questions about each other, then see who guessed right — and who takes the crown.

Common questions

  • “Room not found”? Check the code and make sure both phones are online, then try again. Codes expire when a game ends.
  • Subscription & free trial. iky Premium unlocks all decks with a 3-day free trial, then a weekly subscription billed through your Apple Account. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Canceling during the trial costs nothing.
  • Restore purchase. Use “Restore purchase” on the paywall after reinstalling or switching phones.
