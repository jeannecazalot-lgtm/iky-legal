4. Reporting and moderation

In the app: long-press a player in the lobby or during a game to open their card, then tap Report or Block.

  • Report prepares an e-mail to us with the player's first name, the reported answer if any, the game code and the date. You can also write to us directly at jeannecazalot@gmail.com.
  • Block works on your phone: the blocked player's free-text answers are hidden from you, and they appear greyed out in your lobby. The host of a game can also remove a player from the lobby.

We read every report and act on it within 24 hours. Because iky has no accounts and game sessions are not kept after a game, what we can do depends on what we host: we delete reported content stored on our servers (for example an invitation link and its photo), we remove questions that are reported as inappropriate, and we report illegal content — in particular any sexual content involving minors — to the competent authorities.
