Privacy Policy

iky — last updated October 2026

iky is a game to find out who knows whom best, played as a duo or in a group of up to 10. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. Short version: iky works without an account, we collect as little as possible, we never sell your data, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What stays on your phone

  • Your profile — first name and photo. They stay on your device, except in the cases described below (playing a game, sharing an invitation link).
  • Your game history — past games, scores and what you learned about your friends.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Playing a game. Your first name, photo, answers and score go through our game server so the other players can see them during the game. Game sessions are not stored after the game ends.
  • Sharing an invitation link (only when you tap "Share the link" or "Who wants to play with me?"). To build the link preview ("Jeanne is waiting for you" with your face), your first name and photo are uploaded to our server. Anyone who has the link can see that first name and photo. Invitations and their photos are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Notifications (only if you allow them, and only asked when you use "Who wants to play with me?"). We store a random player identifier created by the app and your device's notification token, so we can tell you when a friend accepts your invitation — nothing else. Notification permission is asked when you first open the app; notifications are only used for invitations, never for promotions. They are deleted after 90 days without activity.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple. RevenueCat checks your subscription status. We never see your payment details.
  • Ad measurement. Adjust and the TikTok Business SDK tell us whether one of our ads brought you to the app and whether it led to a trial or a subscription. They use device information and your approximate location (country, derived from your IP address). This data is not linked to your identity, and we don't use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites without your permission: iOS asks you at the end of your first game ("Allow iky to track your activity…"). Only if you allow it can Adjust and TikTok read the advertising identifier (IDFA); if you don't, nothing is tracked across apps. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ad measurement only starts if you tap "Allow" when asked (asked once, at the end of your first game); you can change your choice at any time in your profile ("Ad measurement"). If you decline, nothing is sent to Adjust or TikTok — and an invitation link opened before installing the app may not bring you straight into the game.
  • Games nearby (Bluetooth). While you are in a lobby, your phone broadcasts the game code and your first name over Bluetooth so that friends in the same room can see your game and join it. On the home screen, the app listens for such games around you. Nothing is sent to our servers for this, we don't use Bluetooth to locate you, and it only works if you allow Bluetooth for iky.
  • Feedback. If you send us a message from "An idea? A bug? Write to us", we receive your message, your first name, the app version, your language and the random identifier of your app — so we can understand and fix the issue.
  • Saving or sharing a story. The image is created on your phone; it only goes to Instagram or your photo library when you choose to.
  • Diagnostics and anonymous usage data. Basic crash and error information, to fix bugs, and anonymous usage events (for example: a game started or finished, a screen or offer viewed, a subscription started), tied to a random identifier generated on your device — never your name, your photo or your answers. We use them only to understand how the app is used and improve it; they are not shared with advertisers.

Other players

What you write during a game (for example free-text answers) is shown to the other players of that game. The host of a game can remove a player from the lobby. You can block a player — you won't be matched with them again — or report a player or an answer: the report reaches us by e-mail and we handle it within 24 hours.

What we don't do

  • No selling of personal data, to anyone, ever.
  • No third-party ads inside the app.
  • No access to your contacts, microphone or location. The camera and photo library are only used when you choose your profile photo.

Retention & deletion

Invitations and their photos: 30 days. Player identifiers and notification tokens: 90 days after your last activity. Feedback messages: 12 months. Game sessions: not kept after the game. Everything on your phone is removed when you delete the app. You can also ask us to delete your data at any time by e-mail — we'll do it within 30 days.

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, and to object to its processing; you can also complain to your data protection authority. The legal bases are the performance of the service you ask for (games, invitations, notifications, subscription) your consent (ad measurement) and our legitimate interest (diagnostics, anonymous usage data).

Children

iky is not directed at children under 13.
