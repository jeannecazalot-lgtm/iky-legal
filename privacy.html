Privacy Policy

iky — last updated August 16, 2026

iky is a quiz game you play as a duo. This policy explains what data the app handles, and why. Short version: iky works without an account, and we collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Game data. Your first name (as you type it), your answers, scores and game history. Game sessions transit through our game server to sync the two players and are not used for anything else.
  • Profile photo (optional). If you choose one, it is used to show your avatar to the player you're playing with. We never browse your photo library — only the photo you pick.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to validate your subscription status. We never see your payment details.
  • Advertising attribution. If you allow tracking (iOS asks you explicitly), the TikTok Business SDK helps us know that our ad brought you here, and whether it led to a subscription. If you decline, this measurement is disabled — the app works exactly the same.

What we don't do

  • No selling of personal data, to anyone, ever.
  • No third-party ads inside the app.
  • No access to your contacts, location, or microphone.

Data retention & deletion

Game history is stored on your device. Server-side session data is short-lived and tied to your game codes, not to your identity. To remove everything, delete the app — or email us and we'll help.

Children

iky is not directed at children under 13.
